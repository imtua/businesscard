# NFC Card Remastered - Hacker Card

![overall]()

<a href="github.com/imtua/nfc-card-remastered"><b>NFC Card Remastered</b></a> is a digital NFC business card that features a LED in it. The copper ring or the NFC antennas actually harvest a little amount of electricity through the antenna from the phone or the device and with that harvested electricity, the LED lights up, how cool is that!

I made this as a reference/example card for <a href="jams.hackclub.com/jam/hacker-card"><b>Hacker Card</b></a>, a program where you can learn how to make your own hacker card and get it for free. It contains an implementation of Near Field Communication (NFC).

**Terms:**
- **Electricity**: Discovered over thousands of years though observations of static electricity and lightning, with foundational principles of **electromagnetic induction discovered** by **Michael Faraday** in **1831**.
- **NFC (Near Field Communication)**: A modern wireless communication standard created in 2002 that uses **magnetic field induction** to power tiny passive microchips just enough to exchange data.

That's how **NFC Tags** harvest tiny amounts of energy from a device, and with that, we'll light an LED!

## Features:
- Runs on **NFC** technology.
- Can store data in it.
- Can light up a integrated LED through harvesting electricity.
- Cool Design! (More cooler than <a href="github.com/imtua/ImtCard"><b>ImtCard</b></a>)
