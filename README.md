# NFC Card Remastered - Hacker Card

![Overall](/assets/overall.png)

<a href="github.com/imtua/nfc-card-remastered"><b>NFC Card Remastered</b></a> is a digital NFC business card that features a LED in it. The copper ring or the NFC antennas actually harvest a little amount of electricity through the antenna from the phone or the device and with that harvested electricity, the LED lights up. How cool is that!

I made this as a reference/example card for <a href="jams.hackclub.com/jam/hacker-card"><b>Hacker Card</b></a>, a program where you can learn how to make your own hacker card and get it for free. It contains an implementation of **Near Field Communication (NFC) technology**.

**Terms:**
- **Electricity:** Discovered over thousands of years through observations of static electricity and lightning, with foundational principles of **electromagnetic induction** discovered by **Michael Faraday** in **1831**.

- **NFC (Near Field Communication):** A modern wireless communication standard created in 2002 that uses **magnetic field induction** to power tiny passive microchips just enough to exchange data.

That's how **NFC Tags** harvest tiny amounts of energy from a device, and with that, we'll light an LED in it!

## Features:
- Runs on **NFC** technology.
- Can store little data in it.
- Can light up an integrated LED through harvesting electricity.
- Cool design! (More cooler than <a href="github.com/imtua/ImtCard"><b>ImtCard</b></a>)
